Prakriti
She.
Her.
I love how she blinks,
I love how she smiles.
Her eyes,
Her lips.
Never heard her voice,
Yet I know what her eyes say.
Tears,
They shine brighter than diamonds.
But she is more than a diamond.
I know her eyes,
but not her thoughts.
I know her tears,
but not her pain,
never held her hands,
never felt her touch,
but my heart knew,
what my mind never could..
Does she knows?
Does she feel?
On those thousand faces,
my eyes search only of her.
A stranger,
yet so close.
Never knew I had so much love,
that my heart could never express it all.
Words failed me,
wish my silence could speak
thousands of lines,
yet too less to express,
yet too less to adore,
She will never know,
She will never see
my love,
my devotion,
how she touched my lonely heart,
how she gave me a reason to live.
She,
Her.
She is like the moon,
far, yet close.
No, more than the moon,
for the moon seems dim
whenever she stands beside it.
So let universe know,
let haven hears my voice
I love you
not for a moment,
not for a year,
but with a heart
that found its home in you.
And forever.
